Field Notes / 001

The conditions are the course.

Wind is information. Rain is information. Preparation is the only response.

The impossible game

Mastery is not the objective.

No player has ever resolved golf. The work is to arrive prepared, accept what the course gives, and return to the first tee anyway.

Note 01 / The read

Take the information twice. Take the stroke once.

01 / Before

Prepare

The work completed before the first tee removes decisions from the round.

02 / During

Observe

The course is never static. Conditions are part of its architecture.

03 / After

Account

The card records a score. It does not record the complete round.

04 / Again

Return

The impossible game begins again with the next prepared swing.

After / 18

The round ends.
The evening does not.

A garment should leave the course without explaining where it has been. Technical performance resolves into a clean silhouette and a quiet surface.
